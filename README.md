# sales_trends
Predictions for Sales Trends

The retail industry has experienced significant changes in the way consumers interact with businesses, particularly with the growth of online shopping, physical stores, and hybrid shopping experiences. Understanding why customers choose a particular shopping channel is important for businesses seeking to improve customer engagement, marketing strategies, inventory planning, and overall customer experience.

This project analyses consumer behaviour using the Consumer Shopping Trends dataset, which contains 11,789 records and 25 features covering demographic characteristics, financial information, technology usage, purchasing behaviour, and psychological factors. The target variable, Shopping_preference, represents the customer's preferred shopping channel, including Store, Online, and Hybrid preferences.

The analysis investigates how factors such as income, internet usage, smartphone experience, social media engagement, online payment trust, technology proficiency, online orders, store visits, spending behaviour, discount sensitivity, delivery expectations, brand loyalty, and other behavioural characteristics relate to consumers' shopping preferences.
